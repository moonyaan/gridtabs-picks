# Personal Finance for People Who Hate Personal Finance

No spreadsheets, no shame, no finance-bro energy. Just 8 small tools that make money stuff a little less confusing — and occasionally, actually fun.

# 讨厌理财的人也能用的理财工具

不用打开 Excel，也不用被理财博主说教。这 8 个小网站帮你搞懂钱这件事，顺便还能玩一玩。

## Sites

### CalcBoxer
- **URL**: https://www.calcboxer.com
- **EN**: A calculator for loan interest, split bills, and other real-life money math. No login, no upsell.
- **ZH**: 贷款利息、账单怎么摊，这里都有现成计算器，不用注册也不推销任何东西。

### Define Your Dollars
- **URL**: https://defineyourdollars.com
- **EN**: Budgeting without the guilt trip — just a clear picture of where your money actually goes.
- **ZH**: 没有说教，只老实告诉你钱花哪儿了——这才是理财第一步。

### ProjectionLab
- **URL**: https://projectionlab.com
- **EN**: The nerdy one — drag sliders, watch a Sankey diagram map your next 30 years of money.
- **ZH**: 合集里唯一"硬核"的工具。拖几个滑块，Sankey 图就画出你未来 30 年的钱包走向。

### StateTakeHome
- **URL**: https://statetakehome.com
- **EN**: Job offer in another state? See your real take-home pay after state tax before you say yes.
- **ZH**: 拿到外州 offer 先别急着答应，看看扣完州税实际到手多少。

### Wealtii
- **URL**: https://wealtii.com
- **EN**: Most crypto platforms assume you have cash to lose. This one starts at $10.
- **ZH**: 大部分数字资产平台默认你有闲钱可亏。这个 10 美元起投，对普通人更诚实。

### Findex
- **URL**: https://usefindex.com
- **EN**: The finance glossary that explains "amortization" without making you feel dumb for asking.
- **ZH**: 理财词典，终于好好解释"摊销""基点"是什么，不会让你觉得丢人。

### PumpProof
- **URL**: https://pumpproof.com
- **EN**: Before buying a hyped stock, run it here for a blunt "trap risk" score from SEC filings.
- **ZH**: 跟风买股票之前先在这里查一下，它读完 SEC 文件，给你一个不留情面的"割韭菜风险分"。

### Read the Tape
- **URL**: https://readthetape.cc
- **EN**: Think you have market instincts? Guess 5 blind stock charts daily, try to beat 11 random monkeys.
- **ZH**: 觉得自己盘感不错？每天盲猜五张股票图，看能不能跑赢十一只随机选股的猴子。大多数人跑不赢。
