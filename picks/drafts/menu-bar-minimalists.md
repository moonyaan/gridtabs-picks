# Eight Tiny Tools Hiding in Your Menu Bar

Eight tiny Mac apps that live quietly in your menu bar, each solving exactly one annoyance and then getting out of the way. No dashboards, no onboarding flows, no accounts — just a small icon that does its one job.

# 藏在菜单栏里的八个小工具

这8个小工具都窝在Mac菜单栏里，体积几兆，只干一件事，干完就闭嘴。没有仪表盘，没有新手引导，很多甚至不需要注册账号——"少即是多"在桌面软件里的真实样子。

## Sites

### TouchGrass

- **URL**: https://touchgrass.land/
- **EN**: A menu bar break reminder that senses meetings and screen recordings, then stays quiet.
- **ZH**: 菜单栏休息提醒工具，能识别开会和录屏状态，自动闭嘴不打扰。

### Pulse Island

- **URL**: https://www.pulseisland.club/
- **EN**: Turns your MacBook's notch into a menu bar hub for music, meetings, clipboard, and weather.
- **ZH**: 把MacBook刘海变成菜单栏中心：音乐、会议、剪贴板、天气一瞥可见。

### Scrolodex

- **URL**: https://scrolodex.app
- **EN**: Hold a key and scroll to jump between windows — skips the Dock and Cmd+Tab entirely.
- **ZH**: 按住一个键滚动鼠标就能切换窗口，不用Dock也不用Cmd+Tab。

### Whim Files

- **URL**: https://whimfiles.com
- **EN**: 9MB, no Electron, no bloat — a Mac file manager that starts and searches faster than Finder itself.
- **ZH**: 9MB，无Electron无臃肿，一个比Finder还快的文件管理器。

### FloatPic

- **URL**: https://floatpic.app
- **EN**: Strips away the title bar and window chrome entirely — just an image floating on your desktop.
- **ZH**: 没有标题栏没有边框，只有图片本身悬浮在桌面上。

### Presentify

- **URL**: https://presentifyapp.com
- **EN**: A quiet menu bar icon until you're presenting — then a laser pointer, highlighter, and zoom lens.
- **ZH**: 平时是菜单栏图标，演示时秒变激光笔、荧光笔和放大镜。

### VolumeHub

- **URL**: https://volumehub.app
- **EN**: No kernel extension, no reboot — a menu bar dropdown giving every Mac app its own volume slider.
- **ZH**: 不装内核扩展不用重启，菜单栏下拉菜单给每个App单独一根音量条。

### HyperPaste

- **URL**: https://hyperpaste.io
- **EN**: An open source clipboard manager whose history never leaves your Mac — no telemetry at all.
- **ZH**: 开源无遥测不需要账号，剪贴板历史永远留在你的Mac上。
