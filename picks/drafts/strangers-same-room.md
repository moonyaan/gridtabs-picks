# Strangers in the Same Weird Room

Eight sites where the point isn't what you do — it's that a bunch of total strangers are doing it right next to you, at the same time, for no particular reason.

# 陌生人的奇怪聚会

八个网站，重点从来不是"你在做什么"，而是一群素不相识的人正跟你同时挤在同一个毫无目的的空间里。

## Sites

### Internet Roadtrip
- **URL**: https://roadtrip.neal.fun
- **EN**: Thousands of strangers vote every ten seconds on whether a car should turn left or right through real Google Street View. Nobody's driving. Everybody is.
- **ZH**: 几千个素不相识的人每十秒投一次票，决定一辆车该左转还是右转，车开在真实的谷歌街景里。没有人在开车，但所有人都在开车。

### Cursor Camp
- **URL**: https://neal.fun/cursor-camp/
- **EN**: No chat, no usernames, no profiles — just your cursor and everyone else's, wandering the same campsite because there's nothing better to do and somehow that's enough.
- **ZH**: 没有聊天框，没有用户名，没有个人主页——只有你的光标和一堆别人的光标，在同一个营地里晃来晃去，因为没有更好的事可做，而这居然就够了。

### ssh.place
- **URL**: https://ssh.place
- **EN**: You `ssh` into a terminal and suddenly you're one pixel among strangers slowly, silently negotiating a shared canvas, 15 seconds at a time, with nobody in charge.
- **ZH**: 一条 `ssh` 命令接进去，你就成了画布上的一个像素，和一堆陌生人每 15 秒悄悄较劲，谁也不是谁的领导。

### Hallucinate
- **URL**: https://hallucinate.site
- **EN**: Drop into a browser-based rave with zero login and a crowd of real strangers dancing around you to the same set. You came for nothing specific and that's exactly the vibe.
- **ZH**: 无需登录直接跳进一场网页版 rave，一群真实的陌生人正围着你跳同一首歌。你来这里本就没有目的，而这正是重点。

### Jamboree
- **URL**: https://jamboree.space/
- **EN**: Share a room link and up to eight strangers' cursors start twisting the same synth knobs you are. Nobody agreed on what you're making — you just are.
- **ZH**: 分享一个房间链接，最多八个陌生人的光标就开始跟你一起拧同一台合成器的旋钮。没人商量过要做出什么，但你们就这么做起来了。

### Deepcut
- **URL**: https://deepcut.live/
- **EN**: A room full of strangers passing around the DJ mic, one track at a time, for no reason beyond being in the room together while the music plays.
- **ZH**: 一屋子陌生人轮流抢麦当 DJ，一首接一首，理由仅仅是大家凑巧都想待在这个房间里听歌。

### Sandspiel
- **URL**: https://sandspiel.club/
- **EN**: Open a room link and hand the sandbox to whoever else wanders in — strangers pouring sand and fire into the same pile just to see what happens.
- **ZH**: 打开一个房间链接，把沙盘交给任何碰巧路过的人——陌生人们一起往同一堆沙子里倒沙子和火，就为了看看会发生什么。

### Earth Tattoo
- **URL**: https://earth.tattoo
- **EN**: A single spinning globe that anyone on Earth can doodle on, forever. You add one mark to a planet-sized graffiti wall you'll never see finished.
- **ZH**: 一个所有人都能在上面乱涂乱画的旋转地球仪，永远涂不完。你在一面行星大小的涂鸦墙上留一笔，但永远看不到它"完工"的那天。
