# 8 Toys That Draw Themselves — Just Move Your Mouse

No software, no tutorial — just move your cursor. These 8 sites turn a drag or click into drumbeats, mandalas, or retro pixel art instantly.

# 鼠标一动，它就替你画了：8 个纯手感生成玩具

不用装软件，也不用学。晃一晃鼠标、点几下，这 8 个网站当场把动作变成鼓声、万花筒图案或复古像素——全靠手感，不靠技巧。

## Sites

### Eigendrum
- **URL**: https://baselashraf81.github.io/eigendrum/
- **EN**: Draw any drum shape with your cursor — it computes the real sound that shape would make. Move the outline, hear the pitch shift.
- **ZH**: 随手画一个鼓面轮廓，网站会算出这形状敲击时该发出的声音，改改轮廓音高就变。

### Geometric Kaleidoscope Creator
- **URL**: https://bionichaos.com/Kaleidoscope/
- **EN**: Drag your cursor and every stroke mirrors instantly across up to 12 symmetry lines, building a mandala as you move. Flood-fill mode adds color.
- **ZH**: 拖动鼠标画线，笔触会实时对称复制到多达 12 条轴线上，边画边长出万花筒图案，还能一键填色。

### Escherllate
- **URL**: https://escherllate.app/
- **EN**: Draw one tile and the whole page fills in around you — your doodle repeats into an Escher-style tessellation before you lift the mouse.
- **ZH**: 在一块瓷砖上随手画几笔，整个页面立刻拼成一整幅埃舍尔风格的镶嵌画。

### Sandboxels
- **URL**: https://sandboxels.r74n.com/
- **EN**: Drag your cursor to drop sand, water, lava, or fire and watch 500+ elements react in real time. Point and drag is the whole interface.
- **ZH**: 拖动鼠标撒下沙子、水、熔岩或火，500 多种元素实时发生反应，鼠标怎么拖画面就怎么变。

### Dither It
- **URL**: https://ditherit.com/
- **EN**: Drop in any photo, click through Floyd-Steinberg or Bayer, and watch it snap into gritty retro pixels instantly.
- **ZH**: 丢一张照片进去，点几下 Floyd-Steinberg 或 Bayer 算法，照片瞬间变成粗颗粒复古像素风。

### Quick, Draw!
- **URL**: https://quickdraw.withgoogle.com/
- **EN**: Google's neural net watches every stroke you make and tries to guess what you're drawing in 20 seconds flat. Proof this genre scales to millions.
- **ZH**: 你在画布上随手涂，Google 的神经网络实时猜你画的是什么，限时 20 秒，证明了这玩法能红成全球游戏。

### InteractiveArt.space
- **URL**: https://interactiveart.space/
- **EN**: Move your mouse and it paints velocity-reactive brush strokes behind the cursor — switch styles from starry-night particles to cyberpunk.
- **ZH**: 移动鼠标，光标身后就画出跟随速度变化的笔触，可切换星空粒子、赛博朋克等好几种风格。

### Drawesome
- **URL**: https://benji.org/drawesome
- **EN**: Technically a toolbar component for developers, but the demo is a real sketchpad — seven brush styles that thin or fatten with your speed.
- **ZH**: 严格来说是给开发者用的绘图工具栏组件，但演示页就是一块画板，七种笔刷跟着移动速度变粗变细。
