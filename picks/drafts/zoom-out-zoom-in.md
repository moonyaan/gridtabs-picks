# Eight Sites You Explore by Scrolling

Eight interactive sites that live in your scroll wheel — out to a black hole, in to a transistor, and every scale in between.

# 滚动鼠标滚轮，滚出八个不同的尺度

这8个网站都藏在你的滚轮里。往外滚到黑洞，往里滚到晶体管，中间是各种意想不到的尺度体验。

## Sites

### Universe Atlas

- **URL**: https://universeatlas.org
- **EN**: One continuous zoom from inside a proton to the cosmic web, 43 orders of magnitude in a single scroll. This is the whole theme in one site.
- **ZH**: 从质子内部一路滚轮缩放到宇宙网状结构，跨越43个数量级，中间不换页。这个合集的主题，这一个网站就讲完了。

### Physically Accurate Black Hole

- **URL**: https://blackhole.plav.in
- **EN**: Zoom out far enough and gravity itself starts bending the view. A browser-rendered black hole you can park in your own room.
- **ZH**: 缩放到宇宙尺度的另一端，连光线都被引力弯曲了。一个能被你"摆"进自己房间里的黑洞。

### Transistor Animations

- **URL**: https://brandonli.net
- **EN**: Zoom all the way in the other direction — past the visible, down to the switch that every computer is built from.
- **ZH**: 往相反方向缩到底，缩过肉眼可见的世界，缩到每台电脑赖以运转的那个开关。

### The Deep Sea

- **URL**: https://neal.fun/deep-sea
- **EN**: Scroll down instead of out — past the sunlight zone, past the anglerfish, past the Titanic wreck, down to the Mariana Trench. Depth is just another axis of zoom.
- **ZH**: 这次往下滚，不是往外缩——穿过阳光带，路过鮟鱇鱼，路过泰坦尼克号残骸，一路滚到马里亚纳海沟。深度也是一种缩放。

### The Scale of the Universe 2

- **URL**: https://htwins.net/scale2
- **EN**: One slider, everything in existence — drag from quantum foam past atoms, people, planets, all the way to the observable universe. No scrolling this time, just a single bar that holds all of scale.
- **ZH**: 一根滑条装下整个宇宙的尺度——从量子泡沫拖到原子、人、星球，一路拖到可观测宇宙的边界。这次不用滚轮，一根拖动条就够了。

### HumanForScale

- **URL**: https://www.humanforscale.app
- **EN**: The most literal answer to "how big is that, really" — drop a human next to anything and let your own body be the ruler.
- **ZH**: "这东西到底多大"最直接的答案：把一个人放在任何物体旁边，让身体本身变成尺子。

### Ohayo World

- **URL**: https://www.ohayoworld.com
- **EN**: Zoom out to the whole planet and watch the sunrise line sweep across it in real time — the largest, slowest zoom there is.
- **ZH**: 缩到整颗星球的尺度，看晨昏线实时扫过地表。这大概是所有缩放里最慢、也最大的一种。

### Worldview Upgrader

- **URL**: https://upgrader.gapminder.org
- **EN**: A different kind of zoom-out — from your assumptions about poverty and population to what the data actually says.
- **ZH**: 另一种意义上的"缩小看全局"：从你对贫困、人口的直觉认知，缩到数据真正说的话。
