# Right Now: 8 Windows Into the World, Live

No archives, no "best of" — 8 sites showing live events as they unfold: a honeypot under attack, a plane overhead, a forecast graded against reality.

# 此刻正在发生的 8 件事

黑客正在探测一台蜜罐服务器，一架飞机正从头顶飞过，一份天气预测正在被现实打分——这 8 个网站不是存档也不是精选合集，画面每一秒都不一样，刷新一下就能看到。

## Sites

### Honeypot Live
- **URL**: https://honeypotlive.cc
- **EN**: A genuine server, deliberately left open, under attack as you read this. Every username and password some bot just tried scrolls past on screen — this is what the internet's background noise actually looks like when you stop filtering it out.
- **ZH**: 一台故意暴露在公网的服务器，正被人攻击着。每一个刚刚被机器人试过的用户名和密码都滚动而过——这就是互联网背景噪音被摘掉滤镜之后的样子。

### Overflight
- **URL**: https://dgrlabs.co/overflight/
- **EN**: Point it up and it names the plane currently over your head — airline, flight number, route, altitude, all pulled from transponder signals as they broadcast. The next time something roars overhead, you don't have to wonder.
- **ZH**: 抬头看不出是什么飞机？它能告诉你头顶那架飞机的航司、航班号、航线和高度，数据直接来自应答机信号。下次听到天上有响动，不用再猜了。

### Stowaway
- **URL**: https://stowaway.live/
- **EN**: Pick an aircraft or satellite passing overhead this minute and ride along in its window seat — actual terrain, actual weather, actual light, rendered as it's happening. The closest thing to teleporting into someone else's sky.
- **ZH**: 挑一架正真实飞过头顶的飞机或卫星，坐进它的窗边视角——地形、天气、光线都是真实的，都是这一分钟正在发生的。像是瞬间传送进了别人头顶的那片天空。

### VeriSky Scoreboard
- **URL**: https://nickleenders.github.io/verisky-scoreboard/
- **EN**: Every weather app claims to be accurate. This scoreboard actually checks — grading the major forecast models against what really happened over the last 30 days, updated as new results come in. Find out which one has been lying to you.
- **ZH**: 每个天气 App 都说自己准，这个看板真的去核实了——把主流预测模型和过去 30 天的实际天气逐一比对打分，结果持续更新。看看到底哪个一直在骗你。

### Right Now, Israel Votes
- **URL**: https://theelectiondashboard.com
- **EN**: Polling numbers, coalition math, and seat projections for Israel's parliamentary election, recalculated the moment new data lands — a 538-style read on a race that's still moving.
- **ZH**: 以色列议会选举的民调数字、组阁算法和议席预测，新数据一到就重新计算——538 风格的战况板，选情还没定，数字也还在变。

### National Grid: Live
- **URL**: https://grid.iamkate.com/
- **EN**: One person's dashboard tracking Britain's power grid: price, carbon output, and exactly what mix of wind, gas, and nuclear is keeping the lights on this minute.
- **ZH**: 一个人做的英国电网仪表盘：电价、碳排放，还有风电、燃气、核电这一分钟各自出了多少力，全部实时可查。

### Congress Trading Monitor
- **URL**: https://congress.kadoa.com
- **EN**: Every stock trade a member of Congress has to disclose, pulled in and posted as the filings land — not next quarter's news roundup, this week's paper trail, live.
- **ZH**: 国会议员每一笔依法必须披露的股票交易，一提交就被抓取上线——不是下个季度的新闻综述，是这周正在生成的公开记录，实时更新。

### Blitzortung Lightning Map
- **URL**: https://www.lightningmaps.org/
- **EN**: Run entirely by volunteer sensor operators, not a weather agency. Every strike lights up the instant it's detected, then fades from white to red over 20 minutes — a planet's worth of storms, unfiltered.
- **ZH**: 由全球志愿者的传感器网络供电，不是官方气象机构。每一次闪电落地的瞬间就会点亮地图，20 分钟内从白色渐变成暗红——全球雷暴现场直播，没人剪辑。
