# The Internet's Fake Physics Lab

Eight browser tabs that simulate gravity, sand, glass, and fire with more conviction than they should. None of it is real. All of it behaves like it is.

# 浏览器里的伪物理实验室

八个网页，模拟引力、沙粒、玻璃、火焰的方式比一个浏览器标签页该有的说服力强得多。没有一个是真的，但每一个都表现得像真的一样。

## Sites

### Newton's Orchard
- **URL**: https://newtonsorchard.app
- **EN**: Throw a planet, watch gravity do the rest — orbital mechanics run on your mouse clicks.
- **ZH**: 丢一颗行星出去，剩下的交给引力——真实轨道力学靠鼠标点击驱动。

### Garden Anomaly
- **URL**: https://dasprinzip.com/tinker/day41
- **EN**: A glass bubble that ripples and hums when stirred — WebGPU rendering weight you can feel.
- **ZH**: 一颗被搅动就会泛起涟漪和声音的玻璃泡泡——WebGPU 把流体和光渲染得仿佛真的有重量。

### Sandboxels
- **URL**: https://sandboxels.r74n.com
- **EN**: 500+ elements that actually react — lava meets water, you get basalt.
- **ZH**: 500多种元素真的会互相反应——岩浆遇水变玄武岩。

### Sandspiel
- **URL**: https://sandspiel.club/
- **EN**: Same falling-sand physics, but hand the beaker to a friend and watch it get messy.
- **ZH**: 同样是落沙物理引擎，但可以把烧杯递给朋友，一起搞乱。

### Physically Accurate Black Hole
- **URL**: https://blackhole.plav.in
- **EN**: Real gravitational lensing math, with the deadpan caption "we have a black hole at home."
- **ZH**: 真实的引力透镜数学计算，配文冷静地写着「我们家也有个黑洞」。

### Firewood Splitting Simulator
- **URL**: https://screen.toys/firewood/
- **EN**: No orbits, no chemistry — just an axe, a log, and physics splitting it in two, forever.
- **ZH**: 没有轨道，没有化学反应——只有一把斧头、一根木头，一次次被劈成两半。

### WebGL Glass Xylophone
- **URL**: https://tympanus.net/Tutorials/Xylophone/
- **EN**: 64 glass bars that ring true notes when your cursor sweeps past — physics you can hear.
- **ZH**: 64根磨砂玻璃条，光标划过就敲出真实音符——听得见的物理。

### Eiffel Tower Catapult
- **URL**: https://tympanus.net/Development/EiffelCatapult/
- **EN**: Real Paris terrain plus a tower that bends like a slingshot — physically accurate absurdity.
- **ZH**: 真实的巴黎地形数据，加一座会弯曲成弹弓的铁塔——荒唐的「物理精确」。
