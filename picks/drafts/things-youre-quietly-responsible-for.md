# Things You're Quietly Responsible For

Eight tools for the unglamorous work of not letting people down: texts you meant to send, growth you're tracking, gutters that leak when forgotten.

# 你默默扛着的这些责任

这里没有效率工具带来的成就感，只有八个帮你不辜负别人的小工具——那个你一直想回消息的朋友、那个你该记录成长的同事、还有那个没人注意直到堵了才想起来的排水沟。

## Sites

### Kinu
- **URL**: https://kinu.care
- **EN**: Remembers birthdays before you forget — a quiet ledger for people you love, not a networking CRM.
- **ZH**: 记住「我肯定不会忘」的生日和小细节，快忘时提醒你——不是拓展人脉，是记着你爱的人。

### LastShelf
- **URL**: https://lastshelf.ai
- **EN**: Passwords, wishes, insurance — turned into a guide your family can actually follow.
- **ZH**: 密码、遗愿、保险要怎么交代？变成一份家人真能照着做的指南。

### Honestly
- **URL**: https://usehonestly.com
- **EN**: Responsible for a team means you're often last to hear the truth. This gets it to you anonymously.
- **ZH**: 对团队负责，却总最后一个听到真话？用匿名方式让大家真正告诉你。

### Assay
- **URL**: https://runassay.app
- **EN**: A personal CRM for the guilt of solo founders — people you meant to follow up with, slipping away.
- **ZH**: 独立开发者特有的愧疚：想跟进却没跟进的人，一条条悄悄溜走。

### Continuum
- **URL**: https://roland.leth.ro/projects/continuum
- **EN**: For managers serious about watching someone grow — notes so their progress outlives your memory.
- **ZH**: 认真对待「看着一个人成长」——记下私密笔记，不再只靠模糊记忆。

### Poppy
- **URL**: https://poppy-connection-keeper.netlify.app
- **EN**: A garden that wilts when you've gone quiet on someone — the gentlest guilt trip, zero pressure.
- **ZH**: 一座在你冷落某人时悄悄枯萎的花园，零压力提醒你该联系了。

### Noah AI
- **URL**: https://heynoah.io
- **EN**: When responsibility means six unanswered replies — an assistant that clears the backlog first.
- **ZH**: 日程、跟进、欠着六个人的回复——悄悄清掉积压，别等它变危机。

### Upkeep Nest
- **URL**: https://upkeepnest.com
- **EN**: The literal version: schedules seasonal home upkeep before the filter becomes a leak.
- **ZH**: 房子不会提醒你该换滤芯——先安排好季节性维护，别等漏水。
