# The Website As a Place You Walk Into

Eight sites where browsing means walking, not scrolling — a portfolio you play fetch in, a gallery made of torn paper, a brand you visit hour by hour.

# 这些网站，你是走进去的

八个不靠滚动、靠走动来「逛」的网站。一个能陪狗玩接飞盘的简历，一条撕纸展开的画廊长廊，一个分早中晚拜访的护手霜品牌。它们都不追求秒开，追求的是让你觉得自己真的到了某个地方。

## Sites

### Jay Ransijn Portfolio
- **URL**: https://jayransijn.com/
- **EN**: The whole theme in one link — a résumé you control with WASD, dog and garage band included.
- **ZH**: 整个主题的最佳代言——用方向键操控的简历，能陪狗玩飞盘、能开乐队。

### ITom Portfolio
- **URL**: https://itomdev.com/
- **EN**: A flat sheet of paper tears open into an endless hand-sketched gallery corridor.
- **ZH**: 一张纸撕开变成 3D 长廊，走在没有尽头的手绘画廊里。

### maikasui
- **URL**: https://maikasui.com
- **EN**: Not a homepage but a place — Kyoto, Osaka, Tokyo, synced to their real-time clocks.
- **ZH**: 官网不是首页，是一个地方——实时同步京都、大阪、东京的当地时间。

### Grace Cathedral Tour
- **URL**: https://vincentwoo.com/3d/grace_cathedral
- **EN**: A real SF cathedral rebuilt from Gaussian splats — walk the nave without a plane ticket.
- **ZH**: 真实的旧金山教堂重建成3D，走进中殿抬头看玻璃窗，不用买机票。

### Immersive Garden
- **URL**: https://immersive-g.com
- **EN**: A French studio's own site doubles as proof of how far browser-based space can go.
- **ZH**: 法国工作室的官网即作品集，展示浏览器空间能做到什么程度。

### Isopolis
- **URL**: https://sf.isopolis.city/
- **EN**: Real SF street data redrawn as a wanderable pixel-art city, like an old console game.
- **ZH**: 真实旧金山街道数据，画成能逛的像素城市，像老式游戏。

### Rooms
- **URL**: https://rooms.xyz
- **EN**: Everyone else here built one place. Rooms lets you build and explore your own.
- **ZH**: 别人都造了一个地方，Rooms 让你自己造，也能逛别人的房间。

### A Dark Room
- **URL**: https://adarkroom.web.app/
- **EN**: No graphics at all — starts at a dying campfire, the rest is built from text alone.
- **ZH**: 完全没有画面，从篝火开始，全靠文字搭出一个世界。
