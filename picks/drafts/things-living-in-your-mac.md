# Things Living in Your Mac

Eight apps that treat your computer less like a tool and more like a tiny, slightly deranged ecosystem — something is always napping, spinning, or demanding to be fed.

# 住在你 Mac 里的怪东西

八个把电脑当成一个小小活物世界的应用——总有什么东西在里面打盹、转圈，或者嗷嗷待哺。

## Sites

### Notchling
- **URL**: https://notchling.zopcloud.zop.dev/
- **EN**: It lives in your MacBook's notch — the one piece of hardware Apple gave you for free and never explained what to do with. Notchling blinks at your cursor, naps when you're idle, and opens its mouth to "eat" files you drag past it. The notch finally has a tenant.
- **ZH**: 它住在 MacBook 的刘海里——苹果白送你却从没说清楚该拿来干嘛的那块地方。Notchling 会盯着你的鼠标眨眼，你闲下来它就打盹，你拖文件经过它还会张嘴"吞"下去。刘海终于有了房客。

### Paw-Paw
- **URL**: https://paw-paw.pet
- **EN**: A pixel-sized creature that sets up camp in the corner of your screen and reacts every time you type or click, like it's been watching you work and has opinions about it. Level it up and it molts into a sheep, a frog, a fox.
- **ZH**: 一个巴掌大的小生物，在你屏幕角落扎营，你一打字一点击它就有反应，仿佛一直在看你工作还颇有意见。养到一定程度它还会变身成羊、青蛙或狐狸。

### Nomlings
- **URL**: https://www.nomlings.cc
- **EN**: The most honest pet in this collection: it eats your Claude Code session tokens and gets healthier the more AI compute you burn through. Your coding habit, visualized as a creature that is either thriving or starving depending on how much you've spent today.
- **ZH**: 这份合集里最诚实的一只宠物：它吃你的 Claude Code session token，你烧的算力越多它越健康。你的 AI 编程习惯被具象成一只今天到底是吃饱了还是饿着的小动物。

### Terminal Candy
- **URL**: https://terminalcandy.com/
- **EN**: Your terminal has had the same black box and green text since forever. Terminal Candy lets you feed it any image and watch it wear that photo as a skin — no restart, no config file, just a window that used to be boring and now has a face.
- **ZH**: 你的终端从一开始就是那个黑底绿字的老样子。Terminal Candy 让你喂它一张任意图片，它就直接穿上这张皮——不用重启也不用改配置，一个曾经面无表情的窗口突然有了脸。

### Vinyl for Mac
- **URL**: https://www.vinylformac.com/
- **EN**: A photorealistic turntable that sits on your desktop and actually spins — tonearm tracking, album art and all — in real time with whatever's playing on Spotify or Apple Music. It does nothing useful. It just needs to look alive while the music's on.
- **ZH**: 一台摆在桌面上、长得极其逼真的黑胶唱机，Spotify 或 Apple Music 一放歌它就真的转起来——唱针、封面图一样不少。它不解决任何问题，就是想在音乐响起的时候看起来活着。

### Cubicle
- **URL**: https://cubicle.run/
- **EN**: Your AI coding agents get their own pixel-art office inside your Mac — desks, chairs, a meeting room — so you can glance over and see who's grinding, who's stuck, and who's apparently in a meeting. It's agent monitoring reimagined as a tiny office full of tiny people who never leave.
- **ZH**: 你的 AI 编程 agent 们在 Mac 里有了自己的像素风办公室——工位、椅子、会议室一应俱全——你一眼就能看出谁在埋头干活、谁卡住了、谁又"在开会"。监控任务状态这件事，被重新想象成一间永远走不出去的迷你办公室。

### boringBar
- **URL**: https://boringbar.app
- **EN**: A Windows taskbar, smuggled onto macOS and raised as a Dock replacement — grouping windows by desktop, previewing them on hover, pretending the whole time it belongs here. It's the one houseguest in this collection that's trying to pass as furniture.
- **ZH**: 一条 Windows 任务栏，被偷偷带进 macOS，冒充 Dock 住了下来——按桌面分组窗口、悬停预览，全程装得理所应当。它是这份合集里唯一一个想把自己混充成家具的"住客"。

### FeelMyMac
- **URL**: https://feelmymac.thehighjack.it/
- **EN**: Your trackpad has always been a flat, numb piece of glass. FeelMyMac uses the Haptic Engine to make it lie to your fingers — felt, wood grain, pebbled sand — so moving your cursor starts to feel like touching something that isn't there.
- **ZH**: 你的触控板一直是一块冷漠的平面玻璃。FeelMyMac 用 Haptic 引擎让它对你的手指"说谎"——毛毯、木纹、沙砾的触感——移动光标渐渐变成在触摸一个根本不存在的东西。
