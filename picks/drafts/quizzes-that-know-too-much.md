# 8 Quizzes That Know Too Much About You

They look like nothing — a text box, a button, maybe a chart. Then you type in your income, your birthday, your GitHub handle, or five sentences about your day, and the page hands back something oddly precise about who you are. No sign-up, no dashboard, just a number that lands harder than expected.

# 一堆看似无聊的小测验

界面朴素得像作业题：一个输入框,一个按钮，顶多加张图表。可你一旦填进收入、生日、GitHub 用户名，或者随手写的几句话，它就精准地把你算了个底朝天。不用注册，不用绑账号，一个数字就能让你愣住三秒。

## Sites

### How Poor Am I

- **URL**: https://howpoorami.org/
- **EN**: Type in your income and get plotted on the real global wealth curve — your percentile, and how many years it'd take to catch a billionaire.
- **ZH**: 输入收入，直接把你摆上全球财富分布曲线——精确到百分位，还能算出追上亿万富翁要多少年。

### Cosmic Odometer

- **URL**: https://cosmicodometer.space
- **EN**: Enter your birth date and it stacks up Earth's spin, orbit, and the galaxy's crawl to show exactly how far you've traveled since you were born.
- **ZH**: 输入出生日期，叠加地球自转、公转、银河系运动，算出你从出生到现在实际移动了多远。

### printme.money

- **URL**: https://printme.money
- **EN**: Punch in your salary and watch an animated printer spit out cash at your exact earning rate — hourly, by the second, whatever stings most.
- **ZH**: 输入薪资，一台动画打印机就按你的真实赚钱速度吐钞票——按小时、按秒随便切换，扎心程度自选。

### Are You in the Weights?

- **URL**: https://intheweights.com
- **EN**: Type your name and see how deep it's baked into AI training data — whether the models have "heard of you," and how strongly.
- **ZH**: 输入你的名字，看它在 AI 训练数据里被"记住"多深——模型到底认不认识你，认识到什么份上。

### How Clanker Are You

- **URL**: https://howclankerareyou.com
- **EN**: Paste a social handle or finish five sentences, and this reverse Turing test scores how "AI-like" your own writing is. Green is human, red is clanker.
- **ZH**: 粘贴社交账号或补完五句话，这个反向图灵测试给你的文字打分，看你写得有多像 AI。绿色是人类，红色是 clanker。

### GitFut

- **URL**: https://gitfut.com
- **EN**: Type in any GitHub username and get a FIFA-style player card rated out of 99, built from real commit data — pace, dribbling, defense, all mapped from your habits.
- **ZH**: 输入 GitHub 用户名，生成一张满分 99 的 FIFA 球员卡，速度盘带防守全部映射自真实提交记录。

### My Breed Match

- **URL**: https://mybreedmatch.com
- **EN**: Answer questions across 44 lifestyle dimensions and it matches you to dog breeds you're actually compatible with — not just the ones you think are cute.
- **ZH**: 回答 44 个维度的生活方式问题，匹配出真正适合你的狗狗品种——不是你觉得可爱的那些。

### DataGuessr

- **URL**: https://dataguessr.com/world-cup-2026
- **EN**: A World Cup you didn't see coming: rank countries by GDP or life expectancy instead of goals scored. Every wrong guess exposes how skewed your mental map really is.
- **ZH**: 一届没想到的"世界杯"：用 GDP、预期寿命等真实数据给各国排名，而非进球数。每猜错一次，都提醒你脑中的世界地图有多歪。
