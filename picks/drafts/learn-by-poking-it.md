# Learn By Poking It: 8 Interactive Sites You Learn By Doing

No lectures, no walls of text — these eight sites hand you the controls first. Drag a parameter, build something from scratch, or search a map, and the algorithm or scientific principle explains itself through your own hands.

# 拖一拖就懂了：8 个动手学习网站，不用看教程

这八个网站都跳过了"讲解"这一步，直接把控制权交给你。调一个参数、搭一颗芯片、查一张地图，背后的算法或科学原理自己就冒出来了——比看十遍教程都管用。

## Sites

### Elevators

- **URL**: https://john.fun/elevators
- **EN**: Crank up passenger flow, add more cars, switch algorithms — watch wait-time histograms shift in real time until elevator scheduling stops being a mystery and starts being obvious.
- **ZH**: 调高人流量、加电梯、换调度算法，等待时间的直方图实时跟着变——电梯调度算法不再是玄学，是你亲手拖出来的常识。

### Chip Builder

- **URL**: https://select.supply/game/chipbuilder
- **EN**: Start with a single logic gate and end up with a working CPU you built yourself — the most direct way to actually understand what "computer" means at the hardware level.
- **ZH**: 从一个逻辑门开始，一路搭到一颗能跑起来的 CPU——理解"计算机到底是什么"最硬核也最直接的方式。

### zkGolf

- **URL**: https://zk.golf
- **EN**: Turn zero-knowledge cryptography into a competitive sport: shrink your circuit's constraints as far as it'll go, and the abstract math of ZK proofs turns into a score you can chase.
- **ZH**: 把零知识证明这种"看名字就想跑"的密码学概念变成一场比赛——压缩电路约束数，抽象数学瞬间变成你想刷新的分数。

### Population Rays

- **URL**: https://imjasonh.github.io/playground/population-rays
- **EN**: Search any US location and watch petals bloom outward — each one showing how far you'd need to travel in that direction to pass 100,000 people. Density stops being a statistic and becomes a shape.
- **ZH**: 搜一个美国地点，看花瓣状射线向外展开——每片花瓣的长度就是朝那个方向要走多远才能遇到 10 万人。人口密度不再是一个数字，而是一朵你能看懂的花。

### Indo-European Explorer

- **URL**: https://indo-european-explorer.com/
- **EN**: Click through an interactive family tree of 400+ languages and trace your own mother tongue back 6,000 years to a common ancestor — historical linguistics you can navigate with a mouse instead of a textbook.
- **ZH**: 点开一张 400 多种语言的互动族谱图，追溯自己的母语一路回到 6000 年前的共同祖先——历史语言学不用啃课本，用鼠标点点就能走一遍。

### Falstad Circuit Simulator

- **URL**: https://www.falstad.com/circuit/
- **EN**: Wire up a real circuit — resistors, capacitors, transistors — and watch electrons animate through it in real time. Break it, fix it, break it again; this is how electronics actually clicks.
- **ZH**: 拖电阻、拖电容、拖晶体管，搭出一个真实电路，电子在导线里实时流动给你看。拆了搭、搭了拆——电路原理就是这么学会的，不是靠公式。

### PCjs Machines

- **URL**: https://pcjs.org
- **EN**: Boot up a fully emulated IBM PC, DEC minicomputer, or arcade machine right in your browser and actually use 1970s–90s hardware — computing history you operate, not just read about.
- **ZH**: 在浏览器里直接启动一台完整模拟的 IBM PC、DEC 小型机或街机，真刀真枪用一遍 70-90 年代的老系统——计算机历史不是拿来读的，是拿来操作的。

### Sunflowers

- **URL**: https://sunflowersgame.com
- **EN**: Minesweeper's logic flipped inside out: every puzzle is 100% solvable through pure deduction, no guessing — plant enough sunflowers and the underlying number logic reveals itself click by click.
- **ZH**: 把扫雷的逻辑整个翻过来玩：每一关都能纯靠推理解开，不用猜——种花的过程中，数字背后的逻辑会自己一点点浮现出来。
