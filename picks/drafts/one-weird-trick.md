# One Weird Trick and Nothing Else

Nine websites that do exactly one thing and refuse to do more — no dashboards, no sign-ups, no roadmap, just one gimmick, executed all the way through.

# 一个愚蠢的把戏，仅此而已

九个网站，每个都只干一件事，而且死活不肯多干一件。没有仪表盘，没有注册流程，没有产品路线图——只有一个笑话或一个花招，被认真执行到底。

## Sites

### Eel Slap
- **URL**: https://eelslap.com
- **EN**: Move your mouse, a guy gets slapped with an eel. That's the whole product, and it has never needed a v2.
- **ZH**: 移动鼠标，一个人就会被鳗鱼抽脸。这就是产品的全部，而且从来不需要出 v2。

### The Password Game
- **URL**: https://neal.fun/password-game/
- **EN**: Setting a password should take five seconds. Here it takes twenty minutes, a calculator, and possibly your dignity.
- **ZH**: 设个密码本该五秒钟搞定。在这里要花二十分钟、一台计算器，外加一点尊严。

### Cat Bounce
- **URL**: https://www.catbounce.com
- **EN**: Click, and cats fall from the sky and bounce. There is no score, no goal, no reason — just cats, bouncing.
- **ZH**: 点一下，猫就从天而降开始弹跳。没有分数，没有目标，没有理由——就是猫，一直在弹。

### Endless Horse
- **URL**: https://endlesshorse.com
- **EN**: An ASCII horse whose legs never stop scrolling. It is not going anywhere and it is not stopping either.
- **ZH**: 一匹 ASCII 马，腿永远滚动不停。它哪也去不了，但也绝不会停下来。

### Down For Everyone Or Just Me
- **URL**: https://downforeveryoneorjustme.com
- **EN**: One text box, one question: is it the internet, or is it you? It has answered this better than any status page.
- **ZH**: 一个输入框，一个问题：到底是网站挂了，还是只有你连不上？答得比任何状态页都干脆。

### Little Alchemy 2
- **URL**: https://littlealchemy2.com
- **EN**: Drag fire onto water, get steam. Keep going and lose an evening to combining 700+ things from four elements.
- **ZH**: 把火拖到水上，变出蒸汽。接着拖下去，一整个晚上就没了——从四种元素合成出700多种东西。

### MapCrunch
- **URL**: https://mapcrunch.com
- **EN**: One button drops you on random Street View somewhere on Earth. No search, no destination — just go.
- **ZH**: 一个按钮，把你随机丢进地球上某处的街景。不用搜索，不设目的地——按下去就出发。

### Blue Ball Machine
- **URL**: https://www.chubbybots.com/
- **EN**: A blue ball loops forever through a Rube Goldberg machine that goes nowhere on purpose. Watch it, don't touch it.
- **ZH**: 一颗蓝色小球在鲁布·戈德堡机关里无限循环，故意哪儿也不去。只管看，别伸手。

### The Useless Web
- **URL**: https://theuselessweb.com
- **EN**: A button that teleports you to the internet's most pointless corners. The whole point is that there is no point.
- **ZH**: 一个按钮，把你传送到互联网上最没用的角落。这个网站的意义，就是没有意义。
