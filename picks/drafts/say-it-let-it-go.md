# Say It Once, Then Let It Go

You don't need an audience, a like button, or a reply. Seven places to put down what you're carrying — a worry, a secret, a message to someone who isn't there — and actually walk away from it.

# 说完，就放下

不用发朋友圈，不用等点赞，也不用管有没有人回。这七个网站只做一件事：让你把想说的说出来，然后真的可以放下，不用再惦记它。

## Sites

### Glass Bottles
- **URL**: https://glassbottles.app
- **EN**: One message a day, sealed and set adrift — no reply button, no thread to check back on. You write it, the tide takes it, and whatever comes back is from a stranger who did the same thing. That's the whole exchange.
- **ZH**: 每天只能投一个瓶子，写完就交给海了，没有对话框可以刷新等回复。收到的那个瓶子来自另一个陌生人——你们做的是同一件事，仅此而已。

### Kind Voice Notes
- **URL**: https://kindvoicenotes.com/
- **EN**: Say something kind out loud to a person you'll never meet, or press play on a stranger's voice when you need to hear one. No profile, no follow-up — just a voice, once, for whoever needs it.
- **ZH**: 对一个你永远不会认识的人，说一句善意的话；或者在需要的时候，点开一条陌生人留下的语音。没有主页，没有后续，只是一个声音，一次，留给正好需要它的人。

### FutureMe
- **URL**: https://www.futureme.org
- **EN**: Write down what you're feeling right now and seal it — a year out, five years out, however long it takes to matter differently. You're not posting it anywhere. You're mailing it to the one person guaranteed to eventually read it: you.
- **ZH**: 把此刻的心情写下来，封存起来——一年后、五年后，等它变成另一种分量再打开。这封信不发给任何人看，只寄给一个未来一定会读到它的人：将来的你自己。

### Dear Aliens
- **URL**: https://www.dearaliens.net
- **EN**: Some things are easier to say to nobody in particular than to the people around you. This collection of letters written to whoever — whatever — might show up someday is a strange, funny, oddly tender way to say what's actually on your mind without it landing on anyone.
- **ZH**: 有些话，说给一个不存在的"外星人"反而比说给身边人更容易开口。这些写给未来可能到来的外星访客的信，读起来又好笑又意外地真诚——原来放下顾虑说真心话，可以这么绕个弯就做到。

### Uncovered
- **URL**: https://uncovered.ink
- **EN**: Strip away the cover, the author, the star rating — read the first page cold and see what actually pulls you in. It's the same instinct as saying something without a name attached: judged for what it is, not who said it.
- **ZH**: 没有封面、没有作者、没有评分，只读第一页，看它能不能真的抓住你。这和匿名说话是同一种心态：只看内容本身，不管是谁说的。

### Scream Into the Void
- **URL**: https://screamintothevoid.com/
- **EN**: Type it, hit the button, watch it fill the screen for one dramatic second — then it's gone, no save, no history, nothing to scroll back to. Sometimes the fastest way to let something go is to make sure it can't be found again.
- **ZH**: 打出来，按下按钮，让它在屏幕上大喊一秒钟——然后就彻底消失了，不保存，不留记录，翻不回去。有时候放下一件事最快的办法，就是让它从此找不到。

### Pixel Thoughts
- **URL**: https://pixelthoughts.co
- **EN**: Type the thing you're worried about, and watch it drift off as a tiny star into a slow, quiet universe for 60 seconds. It doesn't solve anything — it just gives the worry somewhere to go besides around and around in your head.
- **ZH**: 把担心的事打出来，看它变成一颗小星星，在安静的宇宙里飘走，整整60秒。它解决不了任何问题，只是让那份担忧有个地方可以去，不用一直在脑子里转。
